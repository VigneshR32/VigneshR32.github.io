I'm a Staff Quality Assurance Engineer based in Chennai, India, with 12+ years of end-to-end experience across software quality assurance and test automation.

My career spans diverse domains — Retail Banking (KYC/AML), Insurance, Telecom OSS/BSS, Identity & Access Management (IAM), and Logistics. Each domain has sharpened my ability to craft testing strategies that are precise, scalable, and deeply aligned with business goals.
